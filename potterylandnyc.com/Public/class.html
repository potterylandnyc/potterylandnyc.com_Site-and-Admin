Firing & glazes

Your pieces

Your creations need up to 2 weeks to dry before firing. They should be ready for pickup — or painting — after 3 weeks. We'll let you know by WhatsApp, text or call when they're ready.

Decorative (included)

Fired once. You can paint it at home or leave it as is. Decorative pieces shouldn't get wet.

Usable ($25 add-on)

Paint your fired piece in-studio; we dip it in clear glaze and fire it a second time. After that it's water-tight and can be used for food, drinks, as a vase, etc.
