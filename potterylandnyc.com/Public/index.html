1 Firing

Your piece is painted with underglazes, then fired once in the kiln. The result is a finished, glazed piece ready to take home.

2 Firings

Your piece goes through two firings. After the first (bisque) firing, you come back to paint it with glazes, then it gets fired a second time for a richer, more finished result.
